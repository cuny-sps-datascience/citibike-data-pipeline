Cells show confirmed shortage snapshots caught with up to five alerts per update, October 5 to 11, 2026, a week partly exposed through earlier aggregate label counts. Groups are thirds of 99 station areas by American Community Survey poverty share near each station. They describe station areas, not riders. Poverty share is both a model input and the grouping variable. Snapshots repeat at the same stations, so they are not independent incidents. The frozen candidate caught a smaller share in higher-poverty areas than in lower-poverty areas, while both comparison methods showed the opposite pattern. All three intervals include zero. These results describe one week and 100 stations; they do not establish causes.
